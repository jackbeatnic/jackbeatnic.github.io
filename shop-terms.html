Studio shop terms

Jack Beatnic · last updated 15 August 2026 · not legal advice

The seller is Jack Beatnic, offering original digital works as NFTs from this website. OpenSea, TradePort, XRP Cafe and other marketplaces are separate venues and are not a party to a purchase made here.

A listing on this shop is a studio price list. It is not an OpenSea (Seaport) order. Payment is a transfer you send to the address shown for that work, with the memo given on the checkout panel.

An NFT exists on a public blockchain. If fulfillment is marked “from stock”, an already minted token is transferred after payment is seen. If fulfillment is marked “mint on demand”, the token is created after payment — it does not exist until then.

Blockchain transfers are final. There is no practical refund button. Do not send funds to a demo or “coming soon” item. Demo rows are for building the shop, not for sale.

The supply shown for an ERC-1155 work is the supply of that token id on its contract. Extra studio minting of the same id increases that supply; it does not create a new token id. Descriptions must stay honest about that number.

A price on OpenSea or another market is not a guarantee and is not this shop’s price. Works listed on OpenSea are a different stock pool from works offered here.

You are responsible for using a wallet you control, for paying network fees, and for tax reporting in your country. We do not collect email or identity to complete a sale — only the public address you pay from.

These terms may change when the shop leaves the skeleton stage. The live price list is the JSON published with the site. Questions: the contact links on the gallery About page.
