JB Gallery (Solana Edition)

One-time owner step. Connect the owner wallet (Trust Wallet, Solflare or Phantom) and sign one transaction. It creates the single Solana collection Nature & Flowers for all JB Gallery works, sets 9% royalties to the owner wallet, and authorizes the studio mint key to add editions to this collection (update delegate). The owner keeps full control and can remove the studio key at any time. Cost: about 0.003 SOL (account rent + network fee), paid by the owner wallet.

Trust Wallet on a phone: open this page inside Trust Wallet (Browser / Discover tab), or tap "Open in Trust Wallet" below. Trust Wallet extension on a computer: the "Trust Wallet" button appears automatically. Otherwise use "Trust Wallet (WalletConnect)" and scan the QR code with the Trust Wallet app.
