JB Gallery (Solana Edition)

One-time owner step. Connect the owner wallet (Solflare or Phantom) and sign one transaction. It creates the single Solana collection for all JB Gallery works, sets 9% royalties to the owner wallet, and lets the studio mint key on kasa add copies to this collection (update delegate). The owner keeps full control and can remove the studio key at any time. Cost: about 0.003 SOL (account rent + network fee), paid by the owner wallet.
